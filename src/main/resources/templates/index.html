Key Features

A complete certificate lifecycle management system built on blockchain principles.

🔗

Blockchain Verification

Each certificate is hashed and stored on a local blockchain, ensuring tamper-proof records that cannot be altered without detection.

📶

Offline Verification

Verify certificates without an active Internet connection using locally cached blockchain data and embedded QR codes.

▦

QR Code Verification

Every certificate carries a unique QR code encoding its blockchain hash and digital signature for instant mobile verification.

✏

Digital Signatures

RSA-based digital signatures authenticate the issuing institution, proving the certificate was genuinely issued and not forged.

🚫

Certificate Revocation

Institutions can revoke invalid or fraudulent certificates. Revocation status is recorded on the blockchain for transparency.

📋

Audit Trail

Every action — issuance, verification, and revocation — is logged with timestamps, providing a complete and immutable audit history.

How It Works

Three simple steps to issue and verify tamper-proof certificates.

1

Issue Certificate

Institution fills in certificate details. The system generates a SHA-256 hash and records it on the local blockchain.

→
2

Generate QR Code

A unique QR code embedding the certificate ID, blockchain hash, and digital signature is generated and printed on the certificate.

→
3

Verify Anytime

Scan the QR code or enter the certificate ID. The system checks the local blockchain — no Internet required.
